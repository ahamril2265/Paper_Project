## Objectives of the Study

The primary objective of this study is to develop a **supervised learning–based model** for short-term residential electricity consumption forecasting. To achieve this, the following specific objectives have been defined:

* **Pattern Analysis:** To analyze and understand residential electricity consumption patterns using high-resolution smart meter data.
* **Data Engineering:** To design a structured **preprocessing pipeline** for transforming raw electrical measurements into a supervised learning format.
* **Model Selection:** To select and justify an appropriate prediction model capable of handling **multivariate time-series data**.
* **Forecasting Development:** To develop a predictive model for forecasting **global active power consumption** over short-term horizons.
* **Performance Evaluation:** To evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed model using standard regression metrics:
* Mean Absolute Error (**MAE**)
* Root Mean Square Error (**RMSE**)
* Coefficient of Determination (****)


* **Technique Assessment:** To assess the effectiveness of **deep learning techniques** in capturing temporal dependencies in residential load data.
