## Target Variable Selection

Based on domain relevance and exploratory analysis, **Global Active Power** is selected as the target variable for prediction. This decision is supported by the following evidence:

* **Direct Demand Representation:** It directly represents the total household electricity demand.
* **Complex Patterns:** It exhibits strong temporal and non-linear patterns essential for deep learning tasks.
* **Feature Correlation:** It demonstrates high correlation with current intensity and appliance-level consumption, making it a reliable central metric.
* **Application Relevance:** It is the primary metric of interest for residential energy management and grid-load applications.

> [!TIP]
> **EDA Conclusion:** Results confirm that global active power displays meaningful variability and is well-suited for short-term forecasting tasks.
